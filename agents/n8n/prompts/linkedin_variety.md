# Post variety directives

One option set per post, selected by `set_variety_directives` so consecutive posts never
share the same shape. The weight is how often the option is chosen; the middle column is the
band name (`length` only); the last column is the directive the drafter reads.

## format

2 | | PURE PROSE: no bullet points at all, 3-5 flowing paragraphs
2 | | PUNCHY: several very short paragraphs, at least two of them a single sentence
1 | | ONE BULLET BLOCK: exactly one bullet list of 3-4 short items, with prose before and after

## length

2 | MEDIUM | 0.5
1 | SHORT | 0.15
1 | FULL | 0.9

## closing

2 | | DIRECT QUESTION: end the body with one specific question to technical peers
1 | | SHARED EXPERIENCE: end by inviting others to share how they handle it, phrased as a statement, no question mark
1 | | HOT TAKE: end the body on a blunt one-line statement, no question, no invitation
1 | | NONE: end when the argument is complete, with no closing question or invitation
