# Scenes and the storyboard

A scene is one beat of the composition: one idea, one arrangement, held long enough to
read. Scenes play in the order the storyboard names.

- Plan the storyboard before authoring: the form, the scenes and the order they play in.
- One idea per scene. Do not split one idea across two scenes, and do not put two ideas in
  one scene.
- A declared count names content scenes. The opening title scene, when the brief asks for
  one, is additional to them and sits before them.
- A scene has no fixed length; give each the time its idea needs to read, and keep the
  composition's total to the declared duration.
