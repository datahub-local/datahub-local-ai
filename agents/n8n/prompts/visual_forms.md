The form is the composition shape of an authored visual. One is chosen per request:
the hook's default, or another when the content clearly needs it. `auto` pins nothing
and lets the author choose from this list. Build the definition, not the name.

- `diagram`: make the structure or flow explicit - labelled nodes, boxes and arrows laid out so the path reads at a glance.
- `story`: tell it as a sequence in time - scenes that follow one another (cause, escalation, resolution), not one frame.
- `data`: make the numbers the message - the measured value is the subject, shown as a figure or a comparison that settles on it.
- `chart`: plot the series - axes, a scale and a trend or comparison read off the picture, not one figure.
- `code`: show the code - a snippet, a config, a diff or a terminal window, type-led, with the line that matters marked.
- `timeline`: place the events on a time axis - what happened, in order, and the gaps between them.
- `quote`: give the source its own words - one sentence, attributed, and nothing of ours.
- `poster`: make one statement - a single type-led card carrying one idea and no data series.
- `ui`: show the interface - a screen, a scroll or a cursor, with callouts on what changed.
